PART 4

Finding the start of every player record

1 | GARY HUA | 6.0 | W 39 | W 21 | W 18 | ...
ON | 15445895 / R: 1794 -> 1817 | ...
^\s*[0-9]+\s*\|

The search pattern finds lines that begin with a number and a vertical bar. The next line automatically belongs to that same player.

PART 5

Opponent entries become player numbers

W 39 becomes opponent 39.

B, H, U, and X contain no player number, so they become missing values.

That keeps byes and status codes out of the rating average.

extract_first_integer <- function(value) { position <- regexpr("[0-9]+", value) if (position[1] == -1) { return(NA_integer_) } as.integer( regmatches(value, position) ) }

PART 11

The validation block acts like a safety net

  • Exactly 64 output rows
  • Player numbers 1 through 64
  • Every opponent exists
  • Gary Hua appears once
  • Gary’s values match the prompt
  • No required values are missing

stopifnot() stops the render immediately when any check fails. A broken result cannot quietly become the final CSV.
